NovaShop Shipping and Delivery Policy

1. Order Processing
Orders are generally processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment confirmation.

2. Standard Delivery
Standard delivery typically takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.
Delivery times may vary depending on the destination and courier service.

3. Tracking Orders
Customers can track dispatched orders using the tracking link provided in their shipping confirmation message.

4. Delayed Deliveries
If an order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, customers should contact NovaShop support with their order ID.

5. Incorrect Delivery Address
Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they notice an incorrect delivery address.
Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been dispatched.

6. Lost or Damaged Shipments
Customers who suspect a shipment is lost or receive a damaged package should contact support and provide their order ID and relevant details.
