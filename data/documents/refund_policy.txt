NovaShop Refund and Return Policy

1. Return Window
Customers can request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving their order.

2. Eligibility
Products must be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original packaging.
Certain personal-care products cannot be returned once opened.

3. Refund Processing
After the returned product passes inspection, approved refunds are initiated within 5 to 7 business days.
The time taken for the money to appear in the customer's bank account may vary.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Products
Customers who receive a damaged or incorrect product should contact NovaShop support within 48 hours of delivery.
They should provide their order ID and clear photographs of the issue.

5. Refund Status
Customers can check their refund status using their order ID by contacting NovaShop customer support.

6. Exceptions
A return request submitted after the 7-day return window may be rejected.
Customer support can review exceptional cases, but approval is not guaranteed.
